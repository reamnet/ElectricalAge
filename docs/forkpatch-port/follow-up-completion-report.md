# ForkPatch 3.0.77 integration follow-up

This branch starts at `origin/develop` (`43a6a54f5`). The upstream merge was already present. The initial worktree was clean, and the separate Qwen backlog branch was left out. The source comparison used the local ForkPatch 3.0.77 checkout at `b7444a7b`.

## Inventory rendering

Eleven kitchen appliances, the washer, dryer, Fusion Matter Reactor, four standalone fans, and Grid DC-DC Converter now use their existing 3D equipment models in inventory. The model provider selects the existing meshes and materials in GUI context and retains their hand, ground, head, frame, and placed model routes. The converter uses the original ForkPatch `gridconverter01.png` material for particles, byte-for-byte. Data generation removes the 19 obsolete flat models, and the provider fails if a required future flat item texture is absent. The generated output has zero references to `eln:items/missing`.

## Electrical and saved-world behavior

- The original 120 V wall outlet (7968), 240 V wall outlet (7969), industrial outlets, and power strip retain their registry IDs. The 120 V outlet appears as Experimental Wall Outlet in the Electrical Age tab. The two domestic outlets retain distinct plug keys. The tests exercise creative tab membership, opposite-key rejection, and paid appliance links across reload.
- Upstream changed Auto Miner, Teleporter, antenna, and turret consumers to 400 V. A keyed 125 A 400 V industrial outlet and cord provide a matching supply. Both receive descriptor ID 16000 in their respective registries. The 400 V key was appended to the rating list so existing wire ordinals and saved names stay stable. Clients need the updated mod to decode the new key. Existing 800 V circuits retain their rating, and an incompatible saved lead on a retiered machine follows the existing safe detach/refund path. Existing installations need a correctly rated supply and cord for those machines. Hardwired installations also need to account for the upstream nominal-voltage changes. No conversion between 800 V and 400 V is performed by a cord.
- Newly installed legacy-form utility poles accept the upstream modular transformer behavior and 11 kV to 400 V ratio. Existing serialized fixed-ratio poles keep their 4:1 ratio. The pole tests exercise both paths with real feeder circuits.
- GUI packet tests use grounded or wall-supported placement and room for multi-block clearance. The packet and menu assertions still run. Teleporter inspection now omits distance when the destination is missing or ambiguous, so several unnamed teleporters can coexist without a diagnostic exception.
- The AC LiFePO4 charger and two-pole changeover relay now use distinct layouts with the same ingredients. The single-phase transformer pole and 120/240 V secondary pole use distinct redstone and gold-nugget markers, respectively. The reachability test follows actual wire-line and fabricator production routes registered with JEI, including wheel and wafer inputs.

## Validation

The unfiltered NeoForge GameTest run completed **1,927 cases with 1,923 passes and four required failures**. It took about 20 minutes. The 14 cord and ghost cases, eight GUI cases, utility-pole case, and crafting uniqueness check now pass. The four added cases cover domestic connections and 400 V outlet directions. The remaining failures are the two historical comparisons below, JEI reachability, and `cable_reaches_a_machine_in_the_air`. The air-route case also failed in the upstream integration logs. The planner rejects the unsupported bank approach, leaving the bank unpowered. Cable routing policy was left outside this patch.

- Compilation succeeded. All 16 focused unit tests passed across cord state, converter runtime, high-voltage simulation reference, and rotated model hitboxes.
- `runClientData` completed on Java 25. A repeat wrote zero files and removed zero stale files. A comparison against `43a6a54f5` found identical values for all 922 existing `eln-ids.json` key, plus the two explicit new keys.
- The initial focused GameTest run passed 107 of 122, including all eight corrected GUI fixtures and the 452-device CPU `item_draw` case. The full run also exercised the placed geometry and near/far bake path. Static checks resolved all 19 new GUI model and material paths. All 11 outlet and strip item definitions, blockstates, and ELN texture references resolved. The CPU test emitted held and dropped geometry for both wall outlets and the power strip. GPU pixels and interactive client behavior remain unverified.
- The next focused run passed the domestic creative, power and reload assertions for both sockets, the industrial outlet and strip checks, all utility-pole checks, and crafting uniqueness (0 clashes in 1,948 recipes). The two powered Teleporter cases exposed the missing-target diagnostic exception, which was fixed before the full run.
- The JEI reachability audit improved from 192 unreachable of 874 listed to 81 of 876. The remaining failures include unbootstrapped arc-clay and arc-metal cycles, dependent wire and machine recipes, legacy cable-dependent lighting, and missing JEI acquisition routes for some in-world products. Several 120 V and 240 V lighting recipes still request the legacy low- and medium-voltage cable items, while current crafting produces cable kits. Any recipe migration needs to account for the length represented by a kit. Neither crafting exemptions nor synthetic producer recipes were added.
